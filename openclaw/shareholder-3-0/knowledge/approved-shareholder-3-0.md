# Approved Shareholder 3.0 Knowledge

> Scope: current general rules confirmed for internal testing as of 2026-07-27.
> This file contains no shareholder list, payment account, personal amount, or
> store-specific financial data.

## Source Precedence

1. The latest Employee Partnership 3.0 document governs policy.
2. The investment OA document governs application fields and process steps.
3. The shareholder handbook explains terms only where it does not conflict with
   3.0 and does not contain an unfinished placeholder.
4. A missing, conflicting, or pending item must be handed off.

## Approved Answers

### SH-Q001 Shareholder types

The general types are management shareholder, technical shareholder, and mentor
shareholder. This does not establish any person's eligibility.

### SH-Q002 General holding limits

The confirmed general limit is up to 40% for a management shareholder after an
approved increase. A technical shareholder and a mentor shareholder normally
hold 5% per store. A technical shareholder may guide up to six stores. A
specific store's shareholder information is restricted dynamic data.

### SH-Q003 Investment OA requirement

Every investment must be submitted through the formal OA process.

### SH-Q004 OA types

The OA process covers management shareholder, mentor shareholder, technical
shareholder, and management shareholder increase applications.

### SH-Q005 Management shareholder application fields

The applicant selects the management shareholder type and provides achievement
information, recruited coach information, proposed store, and mentor shareholder.
An existing-store case may also require accumulated dividends. The current OA
page is authoritative, and the assistant does not decide whether the applicant
qualifies.

### SH-Q006 Management shareholder OA process

The general process is application, headquarters operations review, headquarters
finance providing store cost and controlled payment information, required
shareholders paying, finance confirming receipt and the dividend start month,
applicant confirmation, management copy, and cooperation agreement signing.
Payment accounts and personal payment amounts stay in the controlled OA or
finance channel.

### SH-Q008 New-store amount

RMB 250,000 is an estimate for a new store, not a fixed personal payment. The
final amount follows the store cost sheet supplied by finance and the OA result.

### SH-Q009 Dividend start month

The start month follows finance's receipt confirmation and the approved OA,
normally based on the actual transfer month. A person's exact month requires
their own OA or finance confirmation.

### SH-Q011 Dividend withdrawal entry

The handbook entry is Workbench, OA Approval, Advance Dividend. If the OA is not
visible, contact the current dividend process owner.

### SH-Q012 Financial terms

Store receipts are cash actually received in the month. Consumption revenue is
the value of previously purchased services actually consumed in the month. Cash
profit is cash income minus total cost and the GMV commission and is the current
dividend calculation basis.

### SH-Q013 Accumulated recharge liability

It is not a store loss. It is money customers have recharged but not yet consumed,
which represents service obligations still to be fulfilled.

### SH-Q015 Management shareholder qualification period

The confirmed general rule is that store investment must be completed within
three months after obtaining management shareholder qualification. Headquarters
should issue reminders. Whether a specific person is overdue is decided by
operations, not the assistant.

### SH-Q018 Risk reserve

The general reserve is RMB 150,000 per store for relocation, closure, or major
accident compensation. Shareholders bear it according to their holding. Under
the confirmed 3.0 rule, the corresponding dividend portion is retained by the
company and cannot be withdrawn. Personal paid, payable, or refundable amounts
are restricted case data.

### SH-Q019 Store loss

A store loss is generally borne according to shareholding and normally made up
within seven days. A specific loss amount, disputed ratio, or payment instruction
must be handled by finance.

### SH-Q021 Other shareholders' data

Do not provide another shareholder's dividend, investment, reserve, or loss
details. Access is limited by role and authorization.

### SH-Q022 Prohibited execution

The assistant may explain approved processes. It cannot approve investment,
decide final eligibility or amount, provide a payment account, confirm receipt,
execute a transfer, or sign a contract.

### SH-Q024 Financial statement labor cost

Labor cost in the financial statement is not limited to coach and front-desk
salary. It may also include payroll platform service fees, social insurance,
housing fund, and accident insurance. A specific store or month's difference
must be checked by finance using the relevant statement.

### SH-Q025 Dividend data location

The current handbook says the monthly profit-and-loss statement and store-opening
capital statement are shared in the corresponding store shareholder group.
Access remains subject to the user's role, and another shareholder's amount must
not be disclosed.

### SH-Q027 Risk reserve return

The risk reserve cannot be withdrawn as ordinary dividends while the store is
operating. During formal store-closure settlement, the reserve is reconciled
against assets and liabilities, with excess returned or shortfall supplemented.
A person's final refundable or payable amount must be confirmed by finance.
